Our Story

Born from Passion, Built with Love

Nuwafana Pizza was founded in 2018 by a family of food lovers in Mogadishu, Somalia, with a single mission: to bring the joy of authentic, handcrafted pizza to every table in our community.

The name "Nuwafana" — meaning "flourishing" in Somali — reflects our commitment to growth, quality, and community. We started with a small oven, a secret dough recipe passed down through generations, and an unwavering belief that food made with love tastes better.

Today, we serve thousands of happy customers, but our values remain unchanged: only the freshest locally-sourced ingredients, hand-stretched dough made fresh every morning, and service that feels like family.
